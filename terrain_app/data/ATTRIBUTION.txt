Terrain Studio / 3D Map Extractor

Default elevation data: Mapzen / Tilezen Terrain Tiles, accessed from AWS Open Data.
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
Full source attribution and license details:
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

Optional elevation source (when USGS 3DEP is selected): U.S. Geological Survey,
3D Elevation Program (3DEP), The National Map. Public domain; courtesy of the
U.S. Geological Survey. Heights use NAVD88 in the conterminous United States.
https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program
Each model's own elevation source is recorded in its metadata.

Default topographic map: map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, SRTM;
map style © OpenTopoMap (CC-BY-SA 3.0).
https://opentopomap.org/about
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
Street map: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL).
Standard raster map tiles are for interactive viewing, not offline distribution.
Terrain model exports do not include raster map textures. Preview image exports
include the active map's credit line when the map surface is shown.

Data sampling, Gaussian smoothing, model scaling, and elevation exaggeration are
performed by Terrain Studio. These modifications do not imply endorsement by
the original data providers. Processing options and geographic bounds can be
downloaded separately as terrain-metadata.json. OBJ, GLB, and PNG files also
contain embedded metadata; STL models do not require a JSON sidecar to open.

Optional map-feature overlays: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under
the Open Database License (ODbL). Obtained through the configured Overpass API.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
GLB exports and preview images include selected feature layers and colors.
These features are clipped and draped over the terrain by Terrain Studio.

Address and place search: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), through Nominatim.
https://nominatim.org/

https://www.zippopotam.us/
https://www.geonames.org/
GeoNames data is available under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY).

Optional mapped print surface colors: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
Forests/woods, open vegetation, bare ground, mapped snow/glaciers, water areas,
and building footprints are clipped and classified on terrain triangles.
Unmapped ground uses the Dirt color; colors do not represent current seasons.
3MF color parts partition the terrain volume. Buildings in the feature overlay
use mapped heights or levels; missing heights are illustrated at six meters.
With 3D buildings selected, mapped building parts and roof shapes (OpenStreetMap
Simple 3D Buildings tags) are modeled; unsupported roof shapes are approximated.
